Vaulting & Sourcing FAQ
How is high-value vault transit insured?
All shipments exceeding $100,000 USD are fully covered under STACKLY's global Lloyds of London marine and transit vault policies, with hand-delivery by senior atelier officers.
Can I schedule a GIA gemologist audit?
Yes. Vault patrons can request a private GIA appraisal review session inside our Geneva or Mayfair boutique viewing suites once per calendar quarter.
What are the design validation stages?
Bespoke items undergo three validation checkpoints: raw gemstone approval, CAD model confirmation, and physical wax ring fitment in salon before final gold casting.
