The STACKLY Genesis

Over 150 Years of
Unrivaled Jewellery Mastery

Founded in 1874 by master goldsmith Antoine de Stackly at 12 Place Vendôme in Paris, STACKLY was born from a singular passion: creating jewelry of imperishable beauty and unmatched craftsmanship.

Through generations of lapidary art, our atelier has hand-selected only top 0.1% GIA-certified diamonds and rare gemstones. Today, operating across private viewing salons in Paris, London Mayfair, and Geneva, STACKLY fuses historical goldsmith traditions with state-of-the-art 3D CAD modeling.

Chronology of Excellence

Historic Milestones

1874
Place Vendôme, Paris

Founding of the Paris Atelier

Antoine de Stackly opens his first private goldsmith chamber on Place Vendôme, creating bespoke tiaras and signet rings for European royalty.

1925
Paris Exposition Decorative

The Art Deco Epoch

STACKLY wins the Grand Prix for geometric platinum & Colombian emerald medallion craftsmanship during the legendary 1925 Paris Exhibition.

1978
London & Geneva Salons

Global Flagship Expansion

Opening exclusive private consultation chambers on Mayfair's New Bond Street in London and Rue du Rhône in Geneva.

2012
GIA Micro-Inspection

100% GIA Independent Audit Pioneer

Pioneering full transparent provenance tracking and GIA binocular 50x diamond laser-inscription verification for all catalog creations.

2026
Digital Atelier

Interactive Digital Salon & Armored Escort

Launching 3D CAD real-time commission estimators, worldwide diplomatic vault delivery, and virtual VIP concierge services.
