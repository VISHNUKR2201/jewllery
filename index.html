The Heritage of STACKLY

Sculpting the Extraordinary Since 1874

Since our founding, STACKLY has been a sanctuary for haute joaillerie. Our master designers, gemologists, and lapidaries collaborate within our Place Vendôme and Mayfair ateliers to breathe life into rare, conflict-free diamonds and precious metals. Only the top 0.1% of the world's natural stones are selected, each meticulously sculpted to unlock unmatched brilliance.

Certified Perfection

Every single diamond undergoes rigorous inspection under 40x magnification with triple-excellent cut parameters.

Micro-Pavé Setting

Prongs and bezels are sculpted under high-resolution microscopes to ensure everlasting gem security and perfect alignment.

Sustainable Gold Heritage

Forged using 100% recycled 18K solid gold, supporting an environmentally friendly and ethical supply chain.
